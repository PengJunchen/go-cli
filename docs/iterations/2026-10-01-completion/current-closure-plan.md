## External comparison and Go-native decision

The source-primary comparison and its 23-line research note were independently reviewed and integrated at `20e952a` in [pi-opencode-design-reference.md](pi-opencode-design-reference.md). It compares pinned first-party Pi documentation on JSONL tree sessions, explicit continue/fork/resume, replayable model-change entries, and extensions, with the shipped OpenCode server/TUI and permission surfaces; experimental V2 recovery design is not treated as shipped behavior. Consult the note for exact official references and its scope boundary.

The accepted Go-native decision keeps this repository's Runtime/entrypoint boundary, durable session tree and explicit resume, shared admission/run guard, captured effect and trust capabilities, and the separation between durable transcript and live UI deltas. `/model` remains live-process only and resume uses explicit current CLI/config; Pi-style replayable model-switch parity would need its own schema and recovery contract. Child-session persistence and child approval/trust propagation remain separate boundaries. Generic provider/action replay is not adopted; unknown in-flight work remains blocked for explicit reconciliation. These are design conclusions for go-cli, not proof that another product supplies this repository's acceptance.
