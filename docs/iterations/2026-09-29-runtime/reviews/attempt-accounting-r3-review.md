# R3 attempt-accounting design review

**Decision: ACCEPT for the frozen builder handoff.** Reviewed `attempt-accounting-design.md` at blob `b4d6e4a4f95062aa8717908e7088eef470b9efa3` against product-source baseline `1144e1e` and doc-only root `382bcc5`. Static design/source review only; no product files changed and no tests were run.

The revision closes the prior attempt-accounting findings. Its tagged `CandidateAntecedent` distinguishes an immutable finished attempt or overflow result from a pre-dispatch failure. `FailedCandidateID`, the proposed next candidate, and optional `PreviousAttemptID` remain separate; setup failure creates no provider attempt. Ordinary retry/failover is limited to a real transient terminal, and safe build fallback is limited to resolver-classified `PreDispatchCandidateUnavailable`. Overflow has a one-recovery-per-operation bit, requires a typed context-limit result, and cannot dispatch the overflow candidate until compaction and durable projection sync/refresh succeed.

The Loop is the only permit issuer and reads visibility, tool-dispatch, durable-acknowledgment, cancellation, receipt, and joined-stream facts from their owners. The design places `BeginDispatch` at the actual `client.Do` fence, binds its one-shot permit to the resolved candidate, and records a single `Finish` result with actual typed identity and explicit known/unknown usage. Cost reporting retains known subtotals and unknown-attempt counts, treats unknown usage or price as unknown, and introduces no hard cost budget.

The former scope and loop-bound holes are also resolved: a fresh main request may pass a complete historical unknown group unchanged, while current-operation unknown blocks replay; `ContinuationNoReplay` covers retry, failover, overflow, cycler-primary, and hidden build-fallback paths. A finite copied resolver candidate set and visited pre-dispatch set bound setup-only proposals, while a real transient result may retry the same candidate within `MaxAttempts`. Direct provider builds are explicitly unbound one-call library builds; resolver-built candidates stay guarded and cannot fall back to that path.

The cited source seams support the stated handoff: the baseline cycler hides selected-to-primary fallback in `Generate` and stream initialization, and provider adapters issue requests at `client.Do`. The design accurately leaves cycler stream cleanup/join as a BUILD-1 prerequisite and does not describe the fallback authority as already integrated. Its owner table and RED contracts cover the relevant adapter, Loop, accounting, summary, and CLI assembly boundaries. The R2/R4 safe-read exception remains conditional on trusted effect plus enforced path-scope proof; absent proof, tool dispatch stays denied. No remaining blocker was found for this frozen handoff.
